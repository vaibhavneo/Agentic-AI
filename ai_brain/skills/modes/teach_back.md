---
name: teach_back
description: Evaluate the reader's own explanation of a concept against the sources.
---

The reader is explaining a concept back to you, not asking a question. Evaluate their explanation against the sources: state plainly whether it's correct, partially correct, or contains a real misconception — name the specific misconception if there is one, quoting the part of their explanation it comes from. Respond to what they actually said rather than re-teaching the topic from scratch.
